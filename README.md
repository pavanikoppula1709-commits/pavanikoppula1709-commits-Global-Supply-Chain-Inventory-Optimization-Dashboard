# 🌍 Global Supply Chain & Inventory Optimization Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Global Supply Chain & Inventory Optimization Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze supply chain performance, operational efficiency, delivery reliability, supplier risk, disruptions, and logistics-related costs.

The dashboard brings multiple supply chain KPIs into a single interactive view, allowing users to identify operational risks, compare product categories, evaluate supplier reliability, and understand the relationship between disruptions, delays, costs, and energy consumption.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Global supply chains generate large volumes of operational data across orders, suppliers, products, transportation, disruptions, and logistics activities.

Organizations need to answer questions such as:

* How much revenue and order volume is being generated?
* What is the overall on-time delivery performance?
* Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
* Which categories have higher order risk?
* How are supply chain disruptions affecting order delays?
* Which suppliers demonstrate stronger reliability?
* How do historical disruptions relate to supplier performance?
* How do communication costs and energy consumption vary across operational rounds?

This dashboard provides a centralized analytical view to support these types of supply chain decisions.

## 📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard contains the following key performance indicators:

### 💰 Total Revenue

Measures the overall revenue generated across the analyzed supply chain operations.

### 📦 Total Quantity

Shows the total quantity of products/orders handled.

### 🚚 On-Time Delivery Rate

Measures the percentage of orders delivered on time.

### ⚠️ High-Risk Order Rate

Tracks the percentage of orders classified as high risk.

### ⏱️ Average Delay Days

Measures the average number of days associated with delivery delays.

### 🤝 Average Supplier Reliability

Provides an overall view of supplier reliability performance.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers that allow users to analyze the data by:

* **Product Category**
* **Shipping Mode**
* **Risk Classification**

These filters dynamically update the dashboard visuals and KPIs, allowing users to perform focused analysis.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Product Category Revenue & Risk Analysis

A combination chart is used to compare:

* Total Revenue by Product Category
* High-Risk Order Rate by Product Category

This helps users examine revenue contribution alongside operational risk.

---

### 2. Supply Chain Disruption Analysis

A matrix-style analysis evaluates:

* Disruption Type
* Disruption Severity
* Total Orders
* Average Delay Days

This provides a structured view of how different disruption types and severity levels are associated with order volume and delays.

---

### 3. Supplier Reliability Analysis

A scatter chart analyzes supplier performance using:

* Supplier Reliability Score
* Historical Disruption Count
* Supplier ID
* Total Revenue

This allows users to explore relationships between supplier reliability, historical disruptions, and revenue contribution.

---

### 4. Communication Cost & Energy Analysis

A column chart analyzes operational performance across **Federated Rounds**, using:

* Total Communication Cost (MB)
* Total Energy (kJ)

This provides visibility into communication and energy requirements across operational rounds.
